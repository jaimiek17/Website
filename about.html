The short version

I am Jaimie. I am 40. I am Métis, a single mom in Tyndall, Manitoba, and I am building this while working as an Educational Assistant.

I have spent four years building online. But reading people, seeing the pattern underneath what someone is actually saying, that I have done my whole life. That part was never a business decision. It is just how my brain works, and it turned out to be the most useful thing I own.

Pattern recognition is the product. Everything else is delivery.

I am also a certified life coach, and a certified addictions and community service worker. Those took real work and I am not going to pretend they do not exist. They are also not the reason to trust me. The reason is that I have lived the thing, and I can see the pattern you are standing inside because I have stood inside it.

To be clear about what those certifications are not: I do not provide addiction treatment, counselling or clinical care, and nothing I offer is therapy. If you need that kind of support, please go and get it from someone licensed to give it.

Why I do this

I spent decades becoming who everyone else needed. Mask after mask, and not one of them was mine.

Some of that I agreed to at the time and then outgrew. Some of it landed on me at five years old and I never had a say. Either way, none of it got examined. That is the mud, and everybody has it.

What I do now is help women see their trueness and strip back the masks that were never theirs, so they recognize themselves again and live in personal alignment.

That through-line has not changed once, through every pivot this business has ever taken.

How I work

I open the gateway. I lead, I bring the tools and the perspective and the lived wisdom, and I create the space for you to understand yourself. I will challenge you inside that space.

You walk through. You do the work, you make the choices, you take the action, and you build your own evidence that you can trust yourself.

I will not fix you. I will not carry you. I will not make your decisions. Not because I am withholding, but because a decision I make for you builds exactly zero self-trust, and self-trust is the entire thing you came here for.

About self-trust

One hundred percent self-trust is not the goal. Nobody has that. The goal is trusting yourself enough to move forward.

And self-trust does not come from thinking. You did not think your way into trusting yourself. You lived through hard things and collected evidence that you could handle them.

  • I can survive really hard things.
  • I can keep going when I do not know what happens next.
  • I am resilient. I am determined.
  • I can protect what matters to me.
  • I am going to be okay.

When a decision does not work out

A decision that does not work out is not automatically a wrong decision.

The outcome gives you new information. Something may need to end, or change, or just needs a tweak. Or you find out you want something different than you thought you did.

What you do not do is turn that outcome into evidence that you cannot trust yourself. That move right there is what keeps women stuck for years.

I made the best decision I could with what I knew. It did not work out. Okay. Now what?

On adjusting yourself

The problem is not that women adjust. Adjustment is part of being a person who lives around other people.

The problem is when the adjusting becomes so constant that you lose touch with what is actually yours. The more you adjust away from what you know, want, feel and value, the harder it gets to hear yourself at all.

Eventually you cannot tell the difference between your own voice and the echo of everyone else's.

Where astrology and Human Design fit

They are a tool. They are not the message, and they are not the product.

I use them the way you would use a good map in unfamiliar country. They help explain why you are the way you are, and what your natural skills and talents actually are. Then we put the map down and you do the walking.

I am not a certified astrologer or Human Design practitioner. These are interpretive frameworks, not science, medicine or psychology. Nothing I give you is a diagnosis, a prediction, or a guarantee of any outcome. You know your life better than any chart does.
