Sound familiar?

  • The marriage ended. Or it didn't, and you're not sure which one is harder.
  • The kids left and the house got loud in a completely different way.
  • Your body changed and nobody warned you what that would do to your head.
  • The career you built stopped fitting somewhere around year twelve.
  • You did everything right and you still feel like a stranger in your own life.
  • You keep asking other people about things you already know the answer to.

None of that is a crisis. It's information.

There's a name for this

Identity lag. Your identity changed first. Your life hasn't caught up.

Most people use that phrase the other way around, like your life raced ahead and you're behind it. This is the opposite. You moved first.

  1. Old life. Old identity.

    Built for who you had to be at the time. It made sense then.

  2. Life strips you back

    Heartbreak. Loss. Burnout. Or simply outgrowing the woman who built the life you're living.

  3. Your identity changes first

    You already aren't her anymore. You just haven't said it out loud.

  4. Identity lag

    Your life hasn't caught up yet. This is the blah. This is the part you can't name.

  5. You build from what's true now

    Not what was true at 28. What's true today.

  6. A life that matches you

    Same woman. Different direction. Built on purpose this time.

You didn't build the wrong life. You built the life you built, because that was the direction you went in. Then life happened, and that life stopped being the direction.

Not better. Not worse. Different.

This isn't about impulsive change. It's about intentional rebuilding. And as you get older it matters more, whether you consciously recognize it or not.

Alignment is the new SEXY.

Personal alignment is when your life finally matches who you actually are underneath.

Where it all began

Something profound happens when you get intentional.

Truth is, you don't need to become someone new. You need to find out who's been standing here the whole time, underneath everything you agreed to be.

I didn't wake up one morning and think, I'm going to become this new woman. No. Life happened.

I'm 40. Youngest of three. I was never the first one picked growing up. For most of my life I didn't know my voice, I didn't know my power, and I didn't know my strength.

Sober since December 5, 2021. Fired three times in twelve months. Single mom of two. I've rebuilt this business and myself more than once, and had a real financial scare doing it.

Life stripped me back. What I found underneath is what I do now.

I'm not healed. I don't think anyone is, fully. I'd be suspicious of me if I said otherwise. I'm living the identity lag right now. I just have more road behind me than you do.

What you leave with

You don't become someone else. You don't become someone less. Nothing gets created and nothing gets replaced. Three things shift.

Recognition

You can see yourself without the layers. Same woman. Finally visible.

Integration

The versions of you that came before stop being things you left behind. They become things you carry forward.

Authorship

Before, evolution happened to you. Now you participate on purpose.

You don't leave as a finished product. You leave knowing how to keep evolving.

Insight is not the finish line

You already know things about yourself. Most of the work out there stops right there, hands you the insight, and calls it done.

Then Monday comes and you do exactly what you did last Monday.

Insight tells you what's true. It doesn't tell you who you are underneath it, and it doesn't move one single thing in your actual life. Knowing you've been performing is not the same as knowing who you'd be if you stopped.

The gap between knowing and moving is where women sit for years. It's the only part I work in.
