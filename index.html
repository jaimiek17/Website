You are not broken.
You are layered.

Every one of us is carrying identities, beliefs and ideologies that society, family or friends handed us before we ever examined them. Everyone has mud. Some of us have more than others. We are human beings.

The work is not becoming someone new. The work is identifying what some of that mud actually is, so you get the choice: keep it, or put it down.

As you get older this matters more, whether you consciously recognize it or not.

Sound familiar?

  • The marriage ended. Or it didn't, and you are not sure which one is harder.
  • The kids left and the house got loud in a completely different way.
  • Your body changed and nobody warned you what that would do to your head.
  • The career you built stopped fitting somewhere around year twelve.
  • You did everything right and you still feel like a stranger in your own life.
  • You keep asking other people about things you already know the answer to.

None of that is a crisis. It is information.

The thing you are actually fighting

Self-abandonment.

You spent decades becoming who everyone else needed. Mask after mask, and not one of them was ever yours. Then one morning you look up and you cannot find yourself in your own life.

That is the enemy. Not your age. Not your husband. Not your job. Not the version of you who made those choices, because she was doing the best she could with what she had.

Who are you naked?The only question that matters here

What I actually do

I open the gateway. You walk through it.

I lead. I bring the tools, the perspective and the lived wisdom. I create the space for you to understand yourself, and I will challenge you inside it.

I do not fix you. I do not carry you. I do not make your decisions. You do the work. You make the choices. You take the action. And you build your own evidence that you can trust yourself.

I am not an expert. I am a woman who has lived through transformations and is ready to share what she learned so someone else can get there faster.
