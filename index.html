The part nobody names

Sound familiar?

  • The marriage ended, or it didn't, and you're not sure which one is harder.
  • The kids left and the house got loud in a completely different way.
  • Your body changed and nobody warned you what that would do to your head.
  • The career you built stopped fitting somewhere around year twelve.
  • You did everything right and you still feel like a stranger in your own life.
  • You keep asking other people about things you already know the answer to.

None of that is a crisis, it's information.

What you're actually carrying

You are not broken.
You are layered.

Every one of us is carrying identities, beliefs and ideologies we picked up before we ever examined them. Some of them are ours. A lot of them aren't. That's not a flaw, that's being human.

The work isn't becoming someone new. The work is figuring out what's actually underneath, so you get the choice: keep it, or put it down.

As you get older this matters more, whether you consciously recognize it or not.

What's actually happening

Listen to how you talk about yourself. I'm still tired. I'm still stuck. I'm still in the same spot.

Still. That's the common thread, and that's what you're living in.

There's a name for this

I call it identity lag. Your identity changed first, and your everyday life hasn't caught up.

I see this every day, and I see it get explained backwards constantly. Most people talk about it like your life raced ahead and you're the one running behind, trying to catch up to a new job or a new title.

That's not what's happening to you. You moved first. Everything around you is what's behind.

  1. Old life, old identity

    Built for who you had to be at the time, and it made sense then.

  2. Life strips you back

    Heartbreak, loss, burnout, or simply outgrowing the woman who built the life you're living.

  3. Your identity changes first

    You already aren't her anymore, you just haven't said it out loud.

  4. Identity lag

    This is the messy middle. Misplaced, numb, flat, restless, and no name for any of it. Your life hasn't caught up yet, and this is the blah.

  5. You recognize what's true now

    Not what was true at 28, what's true today. This is the pre-work.

  6. A life that finally matches you

    Same woman, different direction, built on purpose this time.

You didn't build the wrong life. You built the life you built, because that was the direction you went in. Then life happened, and that life stopped being the direction.

Not better, not worse, just different.

Who's telling you this

My own story

I'm not going to sugar coat this.

The past seven years, starting with my divorce, were the toughest, scariest and darkest period of my life so far. They were also the most transformational.

I'm in my 40s, single mom of two, and multi-passionate. For most of my life I didn't know my voice, I didn't know my power, and I didn't know my strength. That's not the case now, and I'm still learning, still growing, still adjusting.

Before 2021, alcohol was how I got out of the life I was living without actually leaving it. Then on December 5, 2021, I had a profound spiritual moment I can't fully explain, and it changed my relationship with alcohol for the better.

In August 2023 my brother-in-law died of an overdose. I lived with PTSD after it, and the two and a half years that followed were rough. Fired 3 times in 12 months, which is how I ended up knowing what financial poverty actually feels like.

All of it was grief. Not just him. The jobs, the friendships, the life I thought I was building, and the woman I'd been up until then. It went all at once.

Life has a way of stripping us back, consciously or unconsciously, and it stripped me back in every area of the life wheel. What I found underneath is what this whole business is built on.

What I do now is help women see what they can't see about themselves.

I'm not healed, and I don't think anyone fully is. I'd be suspicious of me if I said otherwise. I'm living the identity lag right now.

I'm not standing somewhere you can't get to. I'm the same woman you are, standing on a different layer.

How this works

What I actually do

I open the gateway. You walk through it.

I lead. I bring the tools, the perspective and the lived wisdom. I create the space for you to understand yourself, and I'll challenge you inside it.

I don't fix you. I don't carry you. I don't make your decisions. You do the work. You make the choices. You take the action. And you build your own evidence that you can trust yourself.

You don't need to become someone new. You need to understand who's standing here now, and build your next chapter from her.

I'm not teaching you from the other side of this. I've lived it, I'm still in it, and I know the road well enough to walk it with you.

I'm a certified life coach, and a certified addictions and community service worker.
