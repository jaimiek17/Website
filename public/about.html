The arrangement

How I work

I open the door. I lead, I bring the tools and the perspective and the lived wisdom, and I create the space for you to understand yourself. I will challenge you inside that space.

You walk through. You do the work, you make the choices, you take the action, and you build your own evidence that you can trust yourself.

I will not fix you. I will not carry you. I will not make your decisions. Not because I am withholding, but because a decision I make for you builds exactly zero self-trust, and self-trust is the entire thing you came here for.

About self-trust

100% self-trust is not the goal. Nobody has that. The goal is trusting yourself enough to move forward.

And self-trust does not come from thinking. It comes with little action steps. You did not think your way into trusting yourself, you moved little by little, and you collected evidence that you could handle hard things.

  • I can survive really hard things.
  • I can keep going when I do not know what happens next.
  • I am resilient. I am determined.
  • I can protect what matters to me.
  • I am going to be okay.

And TRUE self-trust is the reason you were led here.

The part nobody teaches

When a decision does not work out

A decision that does not work out is not automatically a wrong decision.

The outcome gives you new information and perspective. Something may need to end, or change, or just needs a tweak. Or you find out you want something different than you thought you did.

What you do not do is turn that outcome into evidence that you cannot trust yourself. That move right there is what keeps women stuck for years.

I made the best decision I could with what I knew. It did not work out. Okay. Now what?

The real problem

On adjusting yourself

The problem is not that women adjust. Adjustment is part of being human.

The problem is when the adjusting becomes so constant that you lose touch with what is actually yours. The more you adjust away from what you know, want, feel and value, the harder it gets to hear yourself at all.

Eventually you cannot tell the difference between your own voice and the echo of everyone else's.

Honest about the tools

Where astrology and Human Design fit

They are tools. They are not the message, and they are not the product.

I use them the way you would use a map in unfamiliar country. They help explain why you are the way you are, and what your natural skills and talents actually are. The map explains the terrain. You still choose the route.

What I do is interpretation, not diagnosis or prediction. I will not tell you what is going to happen and I will not hand you a guarantee. What I will do is name what I see with more precision than you have heard it named before, and then it is yours to work with.

I do not provide addiction treatment, counselling or clinical care, and nothing I offer is therapy. If you need that kind of support, please go and get it from a licensed practitioner.
