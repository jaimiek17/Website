The session

What happens in the 90 minutes

  • You talk. Not a form, not a questionnaire. Patterns surface in spoken answers, which is why I do this live.
  • I reflect back what I hear underneath it. This is the part people tell me they have never had done before.
  • We find where it came from and whether it still fits you or just fits who you used to be.
  • You size one move and you say it out loud, which is harder and more useful than writing it down.

What this is not

It is not therapy. It is not a strategy session. It is not me handing you a decision, because a decision I make for you builds no self-trust at all.

It is also not a sales call in disguise.
