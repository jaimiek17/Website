What you’re probably carrying

You can feel that something has changed, and you can’t explain exactly what.

The life you built may still look fine from the outside. You’re still working, still parenting, still running the thing, still the one everybody calls. Inside it, the old answers have stopped working.

The questions that keep coming back:

  • What do I actually want now?
  • Why does the life that used to fit feel wrong?
  • Why can’t I make myself move?
  • Do I need a new plan, or am I becoming someone else?
  • What am I supposed to do with this feeling?

What we actually do

You bring the thing you can’t see around. We don’t rush to turn it into a plan. We listen for what’s underneath it.

We find the pattern, the loss, the pressure, or the old identity that’s keeping the situation unclear. Then we separate what’s genuinely yours from what you’ve been carrying because it was expected of you.

You come in with a tangled feeling. You leave with language for what’s actually happening.

You may find out you’re not lazy, unmotivated, indecisive or failing. You may just be trying to use an old way of deciding in a life that has changed.

The session

What happens in the 75 minutes

  • You talk. Not a form, not a questionnaire. Patterns surface in spoken answers, which is why I do this live.
  • I reflect back what I hear underneath it. This is the part people tell me they have never had done before.
  • We find where it came from, and whether it still fits you or just fits who you used to be.
  • You size one move and you say it out loud, which is harder and more useful than writing it down.

What this is not

It is not therapy. It is not me handing you a decision, because a decision I make for you builds no self-trust at all.

What you leave knowing

  • What has actually changed.
  • What no longer fits, and why.
  • Which parts of your old story are still true.
  • Which obligations and identities are no longer yours to carry.
  • Whether you need a practical adjustment or room for a deeper transition.
  • The one move that makes sense for the life you actually have now.

Not a plan with fourteen steps you’ll abandon by Thursday. One move, sized for the life you actually have.

What this doesn’t do

You may not leave with every answer. That isn’t the promise.

What you will have is a clearer understanding of why you’ve felt unable to move, words for what’s been happening inside you, and one next move you can make without abandoning yourself.

You’ll know whether you’re dealing with a problem that needs a plan, or a transition that needs room to become clear.

After the session

I’ll send you the You Left Yourself Out guide, so you have something practical to return to after our conversation, plus a short note with the move you named.
