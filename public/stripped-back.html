Everybody sells you the insight. Almost nobody stays for the part where you actually move. This is the part where you move.

Stripped Back is the main thing I am building. It is structurally locked, but it has not been tested with real women yet, it does not have a price, and I genuinely do not know when it will be finished. The problem

The gap this is built for

You already know a lot about yourself. That is not the problem.

The problem is that most transformation work stops at the insight. You get the realization, you feel it land, you cry a little, and then Monday arrives and nothing about your actual life is different.

There is a real, documented distance between knowing something and doing something about it. Insight without a next move keeps you stuck. That gap is what I focus on.

Who it is for

  • Women 40+ in the messy middle.
  • Action takers. If you like collecting insight and never moving, we will both be miserable.
  • Women who want to be challenged, not soothed.

Who it is not for

  • Anyone looking for someone to make their decisions for them.
  • Anyone in acute crisis who needs clinical support first. That is not what this is, and saying so is not a rejection.

The structure

Five movements

In order. Most of the six months sits in the fourth one, not the first.

See

Your patterns surface live, in your own spoken answers. No homework to hand in, no worksheets to collect. You say it out loud and hear it at the same time I do.

Strip Back

Where that pattern came from, and whether it still fits. Some of it you agreed to once and outgrew. Some of it landed on you before you could speak. Both count.

Know

The belief loses weight. This is where your Soul Map is delivered, mid way through month two, so it confirms what you already half knew instead of telling you who to be.

Trust By Moving

Evidence-based stepping. You size the move, you take it, you read the outcome. A move that does not work is information, not failure. This is where most of the six months lives.

Build From Here

You name your own patterns, size your own steps and read your own outcomes. Outsourcing the decision stops. That is the actual exit.

The container

How it runs

13 live calls

Across six months, alternating live weeks and self-directed weeks. You get room to actually live between calls.

Hot seat format

Two or three women per call. Small enough that you are seen every single time, not once a quarter.

Two 1:1 bookends

Thirty minutes with me around two weeks in, and again about two weeks before the end.

Your Soul Map

Built upfront, delivered mid way through month two. Timing is deliberate. Too early and it becomes another external voice telling you who you are.

Pre-work

You Left Yourself Out, the 43 page workbook, comes with you and gets used before we start.

Telegram community

Between calls. The whole cohort starts together, so nobody is walking in mid conversation.

A move that does not work out is not a wrong move. It is new information.

Almost nobody teaches this part, and it is the part that lets self-trust survive contact with reality.
