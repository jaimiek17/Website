The arrangement

What working with me is

  • Direct. I'll tell you what I actually see, including the part you were hoping I wouldn't mention.
  • Out loud, not on paper. The patterns surface while you're talking, not in homework you send me afterward.
  • Useful. Insight matters, but what you do with it matters more.
  • Yours. We find what's actually true for you, then you get bold enough to follow through.

Just as important

What it is not

  • Not therapy. This work can go deep, but it is not counselling or clinical care.
  • Not me handing you the answer. My job is to help you see clearly enough to hear your own.
  • Not another version of you to perform. The whole point is figuring out what was yours underneath all of that.

Answers

Questions

Do I have to run a business to work with you?

No. Business ownership is completely irrelevant here. Some of the women I work with build things and some do not. The transformation is the work. Business is just one of the places it shows up.

I am not 40. Can I still work with you?

Everything is built for women 40 and up in the messy middle. If you are 36 and every word on this site describes your life, you will get a lot out of it. I am telling you who I built it for, not checking ID.

Are you a therapist or a licensed counsellor?

No. I am a woman who has lived through transformations and learned to recognize patterns, and I am clear about this everywhere on this site. None of this is therapy, medical advice or psychological treatment. If you are in crisis, please talk to a professional.

Will this make me money?

I do not make income claims. Not one, at any price. I got burned chasing that game myself and I will not sell it to you. What I sell is the pre-work I have actually lived.

Do I have to believe in astrology?

No. Astrology and Human Design are background tools I use to interpret, never the product itself, and I am not certified in either. Plenty of skeptics have gotten a lot out of the Soul Map, because recognition does not require belief.

Which one do I start with?

The Soul Map, almost always. It is the mirror. Everything else makes more sense once you have looked in it.
