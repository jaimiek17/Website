Before you open it

How to read it

  • Slowly, in pieces. One part per sitting is plenty. This is not a skim.
  • Do not try to remember the terms. Every astrology and Human Design word is translated into plain language right where it appears. The terms are not the point. The recognition is.
  • Highlight every line that makes you feel seen. You will end up with a document that is half yellow and that is exactly right.
  • One section will hit harder than the rest. That section is your homework.

You are probably reading this in a season where life has stripped some things back. Good. The map hits different in the messy middle, because that is when you can finally tell what was yours all along.

The honest limits

What this map cannot do

I would rather tell you this now than have you find out after you pay me.

This map shows your blueprint at the exact moment you were born. Your wiring, your gifts, your watch-outs, the way your energy actually moves. That part is now on paper.

  • It will not make a decision for you. That part stays yours.
  • It will not fix a marriage, a job or a body.
  • It will not predict anything. Astrology and Human Design are interpretive frameworks, not science, medicine or psychology.
  • It will not do the work. Reading it and doing nothing changes nothing, and you already know that from every other book on your shelf.

But here's what a map can't do. It can't watch you live it. It can't name the pattern you can't see because you're standing inside it. And it can't turn what you just read into your next move.

What it will do is hand you language for something you have felt for years and never been able to name. For a lot of women that turns out to be the thing everything else follows from.

Answers

Questions

Do I need to know anything about astrology or Human Design?

No. Every term is translated in plain words right where it shows up. Women who think astrology is nonsense have gotten a lot out of this, because the map is really about recognition, and recognition does not care what you believe.

Is this AI generated?

I use tools to build it. I also read every single map personally and shape it before it goes out. It is not a template with your name pasted in, and I am not going to pretend the tools do not exist.

What if I do not know my exact birth time?

Get as close as you can. Birth certificate first, then your mom. If it is genuinely lost, buy it anyway and tell me. I will build what can be built accurately and I will flag the parts that need the time, rather than guessing and letting you believe it.

Is this a substitute for therapy?

No, and it is not a diagnosis or professional advice of any kind. It is a self-reflection tool. If you are in crisis, please talk to a professional.

Should I do anything before the Soul Map?

You do not have to. But You Left Yourself Out pairs with it well, and a lot of women get more out of the map when they do the workbook first. The workbook strips out what is not yours. The map shows you what was underneath the whole time.

What happens after I read it?

Most women want to talk about the one section that hit hardest. That is what the Pre-Work Intensive is for. Ninety minutes, one pattern, one move.

Can I get a refund?

No. Digital products are final sale. It lands in your inbox the moment you buy it and you keep it, so there is nothing to return. That is exactly why this page spends so long on what it is, what it cannot do, and who it is not for. I would rather you decide not to buy it.
