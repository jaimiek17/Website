# What changed, v3 to v4

v3 was 43 pages. v4 is 50. Nothing was cut except the old offer page, which you
asked to be replaced.

Pages below are v4 page numbers. The v3 number is in brackets where there is one.

## Front

**p2 · Before You Start** [v3 p2]
Opening copy unchanged. The "How to use it" section moved off this page. Added
the scope note in a thin box: this is not therapy, and if what you are dealing
with is abuse, safety, addiction, trauma, depression or a crisis, use the guide
alongside real support, not instead of it.

**p3 · How to Use This Guide** (new page)
The 7-day structure, in your words. Day 1, Days 2 to 5, Day 6, Day 7 and after.
Then the "By the end of this guide, you will have" box, then the four-step line:
SEE IT, FIND IT, MOVE IT, STAY IN IT.

**p5 · Contents** [v3 p4]
Same list, with two lines added: How to Use This Guide, and One Real Example.
No page numbers in the contents, so nothing else needed changing.

## Part One

**p12 · Chapter 04 opening** [v3 p11]
New introduction. Ten questions, ten minutes, and the 1 / 3 / 5 key in a box.
The old "1 = not me at all, 5 = completely me" wording is gone.

**p13 to p14 · The ten statements** (new pages)
The ten either/or questions are replaced by your ten statements. Each one has
its own 1 to 5 radio group, one answer only, buttons sized to tap on a phone,
with 1 2 3 4 5 printed beside them. The key line repeats at the top of both
pages. The score block sits at the bottom of p14: total out of 50, the three
lowest, and the map areas they point to.

**p15 · How to Read What You Wrote** [v3 p13]
Replaced with your three bands, 40 to 50, 25 to 39, 10 to 24, and the line about
your three lowest numbers.

**p16 · How the Ten Questions Become the Seven Areas** (new page)
The table, plus the path: 10 STATEMENTS, 7 AREAS, 1 MISALIGNMENT MAP,
1 SMALL MOVE.

## Part Two

**p21 · Chapter 07** [v3 p18]
One paragraph added after "On one page, you fill in seven boxes. One for each
area. Each box has two halves." Everything else on the page is untouched.

## Part Three

**p23 · One Real Example** (new page)
The schedule example, set as two columns, now and shaped by me, with the
smallest move, what to watch for, and after seven days underneath. Ends with
YOUR TURN.

**p26 · Chapter 09** [v3 p22]
Three lines replaced, as listed in your brief. The body-and-nervous-system
wording is gone.

The seven protocol steps were a run-on paragraph in v3. They are now a numbered
vertical list using your exact sequence, with v3's extra detail kept underneath
the steps it belonged to, so nothing was lost and the steps are not printed
twice. The examples list and the closing line are on the same page.

## Part Four

**p37 · Before You Go** [v3 p33]
Replaced completely. The Pre-Work Intensive, the 90 minutes, Human Design,
astrology and the email address are gone. It is now The Transition Session,
75 minutes, $222, with a BOOK YOUR TRANSITION SESSION button linked to your
store page. This page is built as one self-contained block, so it can be pulled
out without touching anything else.

## Worksheets

**p39 to p40 · Worksheet 1, The Diagnostic**
Rewritten to match the new ten statements, with the same 1 to 5 radio groups and
the same score block. Left as it was, it would have contradicted Chapter 4.

**p41 to p43 · Worksheet 2, The Receipts**
Same questions. Bigger writing boxes. "Is this still right for me?" is now one
radio group, Yes / No / Not sure, so only one answer is possible.
The em dash in the title is now a middle dot.

**p44 to p46 · Worksheet 3, The Misalignment Map**
Was one cramped page of small boxes. Now three pages, in your structure:
what this looks like now, what it would look like if shaped by me, and the
smallest gap I can see, for each of the seven areas. NOW, THE GAP, SHAPED BY ME
runs across the top of each page. No wheel.

**p47 to p49 · Worksheets 4, 5 and 6**
Same questions, bigger boxes, real field names and tooltips. The Advice Filter's
two either/or questions are now radio groups.

## Everything else

Chapters 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, every diagnostic prompt page, the
cover, the worksheets divider and the closing page are carried over from v3 word
for word and pixel for pixel. They are drawn from the original file's own
instructions, not retyped.

## Two things I did not change, on purpose

- Chapter 14 still says "you won't reach perfect alignment" and "an aligned
  life", and pages 4 and 50 still say "manifestation practice" and "manifest
  your way out of it". Those words are on your banned list but they are in v3
  copy you did not ask to replace, so I left them. Say the word and they go.
- Chapter 7 and the worksheets divider still say to fill it in by hand. That
  reads oddly in a guide that is now fillable on screen. Your call.
